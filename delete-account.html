Delete Your Scriptora Account

Last Updated: July 18, 2026

At Scriptora, we respect your privacy and your right to control your data. If you wish to permanently delete your Scriptora account and associated personal data, you may request deletion using the instructions below.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to YOUR_EMAIL@example.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete My Scriptora Account.
  3. Include the email address associated with your Scriptora account.

What Will Be Deleted

  • Your Scriptora account.
  • Your profile information.
  • Your saved scripts and history (if stored).
  • Any other personal data associated with your account.

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained for a limited period if required for legal, security, fraud prevention, or regulatory compliance.

Processing Time

We aim to process account deletion requests within 7 business days.
